# Needs

Need ≠ Solution.

## Confidence criteria (documented)

| Level | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| HIGH | frequency ≥ 5 and ≥2 source diversity |
| MEDIUM | frequency ≥ min_need_signals (3) |
| LOW | otherwise → status INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE |

## Minimum evidence

At least `min_need_signals` corroborating signals in a pattern. No Need from a single trivial event.
